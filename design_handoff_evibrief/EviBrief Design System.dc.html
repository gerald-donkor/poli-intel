EviBrief

Design system

Calm, credible, evidence-forward. Every claim in a brief traces back to classified evidence before a human approves it — the interface exists to make that traceability legible in seconds.

06

Motion

Animation communicates the AI's reasoning. If in doubt, cut it.
Generation sequence
Motion · staged, up to 60s
Reading evidence → Drafting → Verifying citations. Each step completes visibly; no indeterminate spinner.
Match reveal
Motion · 200ms, 70ms stagger
Cards fade and rise in relevance order — each item was retrieved individually.
Kanban drag
Motion · spring 380/30
Cards settle rather than snap; column tint crossfades over 240ms.
Audience morph
Motion · 260ms crossfade
Prose crossfades; shared citation chips animate position and stay anchored.
Guard pulse
Motion · 900ms, once
Soft opacity pulse fading to a steady outline. Never a blink, never red.
Influence paths
GSAP · 1.6s draw
The one cinematic moment: lines trace evidence to the document that cited it.
Micro-interactions stay 150–300ms; nothing exceeds 600ms except the impact map. prefers-reduced-motion falls back to instant state changes. Automatic urgency re-classification never animates or re-sorts under a reviewer.
